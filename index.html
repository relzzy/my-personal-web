[02] FULL-STACK ARCHITECTURE PRODUCTION SAAS

Cue: Realtime Creator Video Review & Social Scheduling Engine

A multi-tenant workflow and review platform engineered for content creators, agencies, and brand reviewers to approve multi-slide carousels and video drafts across 7 social networks. Eliminates lost links, email clutter, and international scheduling mistakes by combining isolated client workspaces, dynamic browser timezone translation, and sub-second feedback loops.

Universal Timezone Engine Normalizes publish dates to UTC ground truth and translates to each client's browser clock via native Intl.DateTimeFormat.
Multi-Tenant Isolation Guarantees zero cross-client data leaks using PostgreSQL Row-Level Security (RLS) policies and workspace-scoped roles.
4-Way Decision State Machine Enforces structured decisions (Approve, Suggest Changes, Hold, Deny) with mandatory revision guards and an atomic resubmission loop.
Sub-Second Realtime Streams PostgreSQL change streams broadcast over Supabase WebSockets to sync live review cards, revision badges, and team chat.
